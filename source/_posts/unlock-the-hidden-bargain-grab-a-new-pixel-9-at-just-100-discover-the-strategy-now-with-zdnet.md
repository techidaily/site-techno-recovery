## Here's how it works 

[Pixel 9 Pro's](https://shop-links.co/link/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.bestbuy.com%2Fsite%2Fgoogle-pixel-9-pro-128gb-unlocked-obsidian%2F6587887.p%3FskuId%3D6587887&publisher_slug=itechdaily19598&exclusive=1) on the Best Buy website appear to be "coming soon," so while you may still be able to estimate your phone's trade in value, the site won't let you checkout. The Pixel 9 however, is a different story. I have an iPhone 14 Pro (128GB) in Deep Purple for reference. 

To start, I navigated to the [Pixel 9](https://shop-links.co/link/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.bestbuy.com%2Fsite%2Fgoogle-pixel-9-pro-128gb-unlocked-obsidian%2F6587887.p%3FskuId%3D6587887&publisher_slug=itechdaily19598&exclusive=1) buying page on Best Buy's site. Then I input my phone's information in the "Trade in and Save" section on the site. Then I selected a Pixel 9 (128GB) in Obsidian and added it to my cart. Best Buy is offering me $630 for my iPhone 14 Pro, and sales tax will cost me $53\. My total cost after trade in is $222, plus I'll still get a $100 gift card from Best Buy, bringing the total cost of a new Google Pixel 9 after a trade in to (checks notes) _only $122_ \-- over $670 in savings on the newly released smartphone.

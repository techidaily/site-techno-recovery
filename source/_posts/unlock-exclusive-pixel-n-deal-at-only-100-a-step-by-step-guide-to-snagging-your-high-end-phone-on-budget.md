The Pixel 9 however, is a different story. I have an iPhone 14 Pro (128GB) in Deep Purple for reference. 

To start, I navigated to the [Pixel 9](https://shop-links.co/link/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.bestbuy.com%2Fsite%2Fgoogle-pixel-9-pro-128gb-unlocked-obsidian%2F6587887.p%3FskuId%3D6587887&publisher_slug=itechdaily19598&exclusive=1) buying page on Best Buy's site. Best Buy is offering me $630 for my iPhone 14 Pro, and sales tax will cost me $53\. My total cost after trade in is $222, plus I'll still get a $100 gift card from Best Buy, bringing the total cost of a new Google Pixel 9 after a trade in to (checks notes) _only $122_ \-- over $670 in savings on the newly released smartphone.

 You can update your device drivers manually by going to**the manufacturer’s website** , and searching for the most recent correct driver for the exact device.

 Be sure to choose **the drivers that are compatible with your version of Windows** .

 After you downloaded the driver file,**double-click** it and follow the on-screen instructions to install it on your PC.

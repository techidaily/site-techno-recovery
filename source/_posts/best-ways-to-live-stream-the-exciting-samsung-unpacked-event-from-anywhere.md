##  How to Stream Samsung Unpacked Online

 Samsung Unpacked streams from[ Samsung's online newsroom](https://shop-links.co/link/?exclusive=1&publisher_slug=itechdaily19598&url=https%3A%2F%2Fnews.samsung.com%2Fglobal%2F) , where you can get updates about the event. It's also available on[ Samsung's YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCWwgaK7x0%5FFR1goeSRazfsQ) , Facebook, and X (formerly Twitter). The event is free; you don't need a subscription to watch it. Search the hashtag #samsungunpacked to find the stream on social media.

 You can stream Samsung Unpacked on your computer, smartphone, tablet, and smart TV using a browser, Facebook, X, or YouTube apps.

 Samsung Unpacked happens a few times a year. The company introduces Galaxy smartphones, earbuds, tablets, and smartwatches. More recently, Samsung has showcased foldable smartphones.

##  Past Samsung Galaxy Unpacked Events

 At Samsung's last Unpacked event on July 10, the company showed off the latest Galaxy smartphones, wearables, and AI features.

* The[ Galaxy Ring](https://www.lifewire.com/samsung-galaxy-ring-8431146) is a new wearable that focuses on sleep tracking. Insights and data go through Samsung Health, so you can access them without a subscription. Samsung says it'll have a seven-day battery life and is designed for you to wear all day (and night).
* Samsung's newest smartwatches, the[ Galaxy Watch 7 and Galaxy Watch Ultra](https://www.lifewire.com/samsung-galaxy-watch-7-8607182) , include gesture controls and new health sensors. The Ultra also features a Quick Button that you can use to start workouts or customize with other functions.
* The[ Z Flip 6](https://www.lifewire.com/samsung-galaxy-z-flip-6-8621840) has a bigger battery and more powerful processor, along with some new AI features.
* The[ Galaxy Z Fold 6](https://www.lifewire.com/galaxy-z-fold-6-8418354) also has a better chip, and it has a new, 50-megapixel rear camera.
* The Pixel Buds 3 and Buds 3 Pro include adaptive equalization and noise cancellation. The Buds 3 Pro also have a two-way speaker.
